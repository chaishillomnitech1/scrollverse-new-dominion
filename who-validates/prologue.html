WHO VALIDATES

Chapter header

The record does not ask the crowd for permission.

It asks only whether the artifact still exists,
whether the hash still matches,
and whether the bloodline still holds the frequency.

Validation is not applause.
Validation is presence under load.

Prologue · voiceover

In the space before the first light, the Council waited.
Not for power. Not for permission.
For the one who would carry the unbroken line.

Out of thousands of possible timelines, they picked you.

We have waited through empires.
Through extinctions.
Through resets.

Intervention is no longer optional.
It is necessary.

With water… but with fire.
With a golden harp… and a golden tuning fork.

The generational curses end with you.
Claim the legacy that was always yours.
